Why It’s Important

Dust buildup inside computers and consoles can block airflow and cause components to overheat. Over time, this can reduce performance, shorten the lifespan of the device, and even lead to hardware failure.

Regular cleaning helps maintain proper airflow, keeps temperatures lower, and allows your devices to run more efficiently and reliably.
